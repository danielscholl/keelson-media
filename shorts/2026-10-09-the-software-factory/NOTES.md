# the-software-factory (FIG. 14)

**Concept (what, why, how):** what: a swarm (the swarm rib) is a lead agent that owns the outcome, workers it spawns as the work calls for them, and a channel they coordinate in; Factory mode drops turn and clock budgets so it runs while work keeps landing. Why: the backlog from FIG. 13 needs working the way a team would, with someone other than the writer reading each change before it lands. How: the factory prompt asks for plan critique before building, a design critic judging every visible change from screenshots, and an approval by a non-writer before every merge; writers each work in their own git worktree and the lead drives the beads tracker through cross-rib grants.
