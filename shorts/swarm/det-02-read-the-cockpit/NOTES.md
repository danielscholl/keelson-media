# read-the-cockpit (DET. 02)

**Concept (what, why, how):** what: the live card and the ended view: one face per agent, four meters toward the plan's ceilings, the Timeline and Map, then Outcome, Agents, Spend, Produced, Activity and About. Why: the channel is the record; the cockpit is what you check at a glance. How: each face shows its state: a double ring for the lead, a dashed plus for an open seat, a bright ring on a turn, an amber dot when messages wait, faded when idle.

**Structure:** the opener with "Swarm detail two. Read the cockpit." and the problem; one legend board, built a face at a time; then one real Crew run, followed: launcher on Crew, the lead alone with four open seats, four faces arriving, amber dots on the two the lead addressed, the lead fading idle, then waking with an amber dot when a worker names it (tight zoom on the face row), the meters, the Map; the ended view (Outcome, Spend, Produced, Activity, About, Agents); a record-and-glance board quoting four real channel lines, with a DET. 03 banner; the closer with DETAIL 02 · SWARM · ACT I.

**Capture:** the demo stage (`~/keelson-demo/.keelson`, port 7979). ClickClack opened before Start. One real Crew swarm, stsog, no project, started from the launcher by `source/rec-crew.mjs` (DET. 01's steerable recorder on Crew). Done in 99 s, 22 turns, 158k fresh tokens, $0.6404 list price. The four quoted channel lines on the glance board are verbatim from its Conversation list. `thumb: 44.0` in the manifest puts the thumbnail on all five faces with two amber dots.

**Weak:**
- 128 s, over the ~100 s the storyboard gave it, though inside the series' 100 to 130 s.
- The money shot is split in two. The storyboard wanted a face going amber while the Map draws the edge; in this run the busy minute was over before I switched to Map, so the amber-then-lit wake is shown on the Timeline view and the Map is shown afterwards, fully drawn.
- The "bright ring" busy state is a subtle glow at phone size; the legend board carries it more than the footage does.
- The tabs line runs a second or two ahead of the Agents click.
- Workers ran so fast (most turns 2 to 8 s) that a live swarm mostly looks idle with a busy lead; a slower question or a deep worker model would show more pulsing.
