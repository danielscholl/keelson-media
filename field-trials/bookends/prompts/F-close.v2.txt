Locked-off close cinematic shot just above a pale blue-white blueprint map lying flat on a dark wooden drafting table in warm late-afternoon light. A polished brass surveyor's plumb bob hangs from a fine taut cord that runs straight up out of frame; the whole bob stays fully in frame the entire time, low over the paper. It swings in a small slow arc that shrinks with every pass until its pointed tip hangs perfectly still a finger's width directly above a small brass circle with a crosshair drawn at the center of the map. Its soft shadow settles on the paper. Calm, precise, quietly satisfying, shallow depth of field, palette of navy, brass and off-white. The last two seconds are calm and still. No people, no hands, no text, no letters, no numbers, no logos.
