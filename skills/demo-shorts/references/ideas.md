# Idea palette

Raw material for choosing differently each time. Mix freely; none of these is a template, and inventing a new one is better than reusing an old one.

## Angles (what the short is about)

- **One capability, one proof.** A single feature shown working end to end.
- **Before and after.** The manual way (sped up, tedious) against the Keelson way.
- **Under the hood.** Start at the result, then peel back: the DAG, the trace, the SQLite row.
- **Failure and recovery.** Something breaks mid-run; show resume, retry, or an approval gate catching it.
- **A day in 60 seconds.** Several quick real uses stitched with hard cuts.
- **Agent drives Keelson.** A coding agent (Claude Code, Copilot) calls Keelson over MCP; split screen of the agent and the live run.
- **Build it live.** Write a tiny workflow YAML, run it, watch it appear.
- **Question hook.** Open on a question a developer actually has ("what happens when my laptop sleeps mid-run?") and answer it on screen.

## Structures

- Linear walkthrough (step 1, 2, 3).
- Cold open on the money shot, then rewind to how we got there.
- Split screen or picture-in-picture: terminal and browser at once, or agent and run.
- Countdown or checklist overlay that ticks as each beat lands.
- Single continuous take with zooms instead of cuts.
- Montage of hard cuts on the beat of the music.

## Openers

- Straight into the product, no title (often strongest on X).
- One bold line of text on a dark field, then cut.
- Generated establishing shot (Sora) with a title overlay. Theme ideas: shipbuilding and keels, blueprints, assembly lines, relay races, circuit traces.
- The problem first: a messy terminal, a failing script, a question.

## Visual devices

- Zoom or pan to the region that matters (essential for browser UIs on a phone).
- Highlight box or spotlight dimming everything except one element.
- Speed ramps: fast through waiting, real time for the moment that matters.
- Callout labels pointing at UI parts.
- Kinetic captions (word by word) versus sentence captions versus none.
- Generated still backdrops (gpt-image, FLUX, MAI-Image) behind a floating window capture.

## Voice and sound

- Harper (MAI-Voice-2.1) is the default. Alternatives auditioned: Jasper, Grant, Ethan, Olivia, Iris (MAI); Andrew, Ava, Brian (Dragon HD); Andrew, Phoebe, Caleb, Brian (Dragon HD Omni); cedar or marin via gpt-4o-mini-tts (takes plain-English direction).
- Two voices in dialogue (a question asked, a question answered).
- No narration: music plus kinetic text, for a montage.
- Sora clips carry their own ambient audio, which works as a sound bed under an opener.

## Aspect and length

- 16:9 1080p for X timeline and YouTube.
- 9:16 1080x1920 for Shorts, Reels, TikTok: needs its own layout (stacked panes, tight crops), not a letterboxed 16:9.
- 1:1 for LinkedIn feeds.
- 15, 30, or 60 seconds. Shorter forces a sharper concept.
